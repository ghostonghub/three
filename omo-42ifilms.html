2.2.2. Major Historical & Prophetic Films

  • The Message (1976) – Also known as Al-Risalah, depicting the life of Prophet Muhammad.
  • Muhammad: The Messenger of God – A historical drama about the Prophet's life in Mecca.
  • Kingdom of Heaven – Focuses on Salahuddin Ayyubi (Saladin) during the Crusades; widely watched in Urdu/Hindi.
  • The Kingdom of Solomon (Molke Soleiman) – An Iranian film about Prophet Sulaiman.
  • Lion of the Desert – Depicts the life of Omar Mukhtar.
  • Bilal: A New Breed of Hero – Animated film about the companion Bilal.
  • FETIH (1453) / The Conquest – Chronicles Sultan Mehmet II’s capture of Constantinople.
  • Hussein, Who Said No (Rastakhiz) – Depicts the events leading to the martyrdom of Imam Hussain.
  • Imam Ali (The Martyr of Kufa) – Covers the life and martyrdom of Imam Ali.
  • The Tale of Heavens – A film about the biography and wisdom of Hazrat Ali.
  • His Only Son – A dramatization of Abraham’s test.
  • Il Messia – Depicts the life of Jesus Christ.
  • Maste eshgh (Intoxicated by Love) – Focuses on the life of Rumi and Shams Tabrizi.
  • Bohlol Dana – A historical film about a sage from Baghdad.
  • Black Gold (Day of the Falcon) – Set in the 1930s Arab oil boom.
  • Fath Al-Andalus – Chronicles Tariq bin Ziyad’s conquest of Andalusia.
  • Men of Anjolos (Ashab-e-Kahf) – The story of the Companions of the Cave.
  • Under the Protection of Ka'bah (Di Bawah Lindungan Ka'bah) – A drama about faith and romance.
  • About Elly – An Iranian drama involving social complexities.
  • The Chosen – A series/drama about the life of Jesus.
