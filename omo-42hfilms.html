Based on the available data, the most notable Hindi devotional and spiritual films include:
  • 1. Shirdi Ke Sai Baba (1977): A widely regarded touching biographical film on the life of Sai Baba, starring Manoj Kumar and Prem Nath.
  • 2. Sant Tukaram (1936): Produced by V. Shantaram, this film was the first Indian movie to receive international recognition and won awards at the Venice Film Festival.
  • 3. Meera (1979): A highly successful spiritual film featuring the hit song "Main To Arti Utaru," depicting the life of the mystic poet saint Meera.
  • 4. Adi Shankaracharya (1983): The first Sanskrit film, this biographical movie details the life of the great philosopher who revived Vedanta philosophy.
  • 5. Sant Gyaneshwar: A Marathi-language film often cited in spiritual lists, focusing on the life of the saint Gyaneshwar.
  • 6. Swami Vivekananda (1998): A biographical film highlighting the life of the monk who spread Vedanta and Hindu spirituality globally.
  • 7. Ramayana: The Legend of Prince Rama (1992): An animated adaptation emphasizing dharma, loyalty, and righteousness in Lord Rama’s journey.
  • 8. Bhagavad Gita (1993): A film that visualizes the teachings of the Gita, focusing on Krishna’s guidance to Arjuna.
  • 9. Jai Santoshi Maa (1975): An iconic devotional film promoting the worship of Goddess Santoshi Mata through the story of Satyavati.
  • 10. Hanuman (2005): An animated film depicting the life of Lord Hanuman from his childhood to the war against Ravana.
  • Other mentioned titles with spiritual or saintly themes include Sant Tuka Ram (2012) (Marathi), My Friend Ganesha, Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu, and early classics like Raja Harishchandra (1913/1931) and Maya Machhindra (1932).
