2.1.2. A

2.1.2.1. Introduction
  • Based on the available search context, there are not 25 Hindi or English films specifically dedicated to Jainism. The filmography for Jainism is significantly smaller than that for Hindu mythology, consisting primarily of a few key documentaries and recent independent releases. The following are the notable English and Hindi films on Jainism identified in current records:
2.1.2.2. List
  • Ahimsa: Non-Violence (1987)
    • This English-language documentary, directed by Michael Tobias, is historically significant as the first film to chronicle the world of Jainism. Featuring The Dalai Lama, it explores the religion's core tenets of vegetarianism and non-violence. It holds a high IMDb rating of 8.9.
  • 1080: The Legacy of Mahaveer (Upcoming)
    • This is an upcoming Hindi film directed by Surendra Pal. The teaser has been released, aiming to illuminate the essence of Jainism and the legacy of Lord Mahavira. It represents a rare instance of a narrative feature film focused on Jain themes in Bollywood.
  • Shri Mahavir Jii (2024)
    • A Hindi film released in 2024, described as a true story based on an incredible Jain pilgrimage in Rajasthan. It is one of the few recent narrative films centering on Jain subjects.
  • Mahavir & His Heritage (1975)
    • An English-language film running 21 minutes that details the history of Jainism, focusing on Lord Mahavira as the last of the 24 Tirthankaras and his quest for Nirvana.
  • The Frontiers of Peace: Jainism In India (1986)
    • This English documentary explores the interrelationship between the four elements of the Jain "Sangh" (community): monks, nuns, laymen, and laywomen. Set in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, it establishes central religious tenets like ahimsa (non-violence).
  • Mahamastakabhishek - A Unique Event (1981)
    • A 15-minute English film documenting the Mahamastakabhisheka (great head anointing ceremony) of the Lord Bahubali statue at Shravan Belgola, Karnataka, which occurred on February 9, 1981.
2.1.2.3. Other Notable Mentions & Short Films
  • Bhagwan Mahavir – Short Film: A production by Parasdham depicting Mahavir Swami from a unique perspective, often presented in parts.
  • Introduction to Jainism: A short primer featuring Varun Soni from the University of Southern California, originally aired on the Oprah Winfrey Network.
  • Rajgriha: A film recording the ancient glory of Rajgir, closely associated with the life and teachings of Lord Mahavira.
2.1.2.4. EndNotes, Misc
  • Note on Availability: Unlike Hindu mythological films which number in the hundreds, Jain cinema is largely comprised of documentaries, short films, and community-produced content. Many of the titles listed above (particularly the 1970s-80s documentaries) are archived by organizations like the Ahimsa Foundation rather than being commercially available on major streaming platforms.
